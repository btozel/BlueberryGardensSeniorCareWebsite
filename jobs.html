Caregiver Position at Blueberry Gardens Senior Care

Blueberry Gardens Senior Care is seeking a compassionate and reliable caregiver to join our team. We are dedicated to providing the utmost-quality care to our senior residents in a supportive and respectful environment.

Requirements
  • Active HCA (Home Care Aide) or CNA (Certified Nursing Assistant) license
  • Ability to pass a background check
  • Current certifications in:
    • CPR / First Aid
    • Dementia Care
    • Mental Health
    • Nurse Delegation
    • Nurse Delegation with Diabetes
Position Details
  • Full-time / Part-time opportunities available
  • Live-in arrangement may be negotiated
  • Wages are competitive and negotiable based on experience and qualifications
